SHATTERED tour ticketing history

The initial plan set Kooky sales and upgrades for February 13 at 5 PM CET and general sales for February 16 at the same time. Ticketing was delayed amid fee, tax and cost adjustments.

A later recap listed February 20 general sales for Huskvarna, Paris, Helsinki, London, Belgrade and Frankfurt, and February 23 for Milan, Bordeaux and Berlin. Bordeaux and Berlin also experienced ticket-preparation delays.

Early Bird discounts and official SHATTERED European-tour merchandise were introduced separately. The displayed discounted prices excluded platform fees and local taxes.
