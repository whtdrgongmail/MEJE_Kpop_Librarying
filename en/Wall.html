Wall

Walls serve as WABIF and Multiverse backdrops and as the subject of decorating Singing Beetle’s office.

1VERSEKEYWORD

Photo backdrops and the office story

WABIF photos show people leaning against walls with sunlight and shadows; the Visualizer includes concrete walls and steps. Walls also frame SEOK’s football photo and AITO’s headphone photo in Multiverse. The SB BOYZ UNPLUGGED wall-painting episode introduces the founder and artist painting Singing Beetle’s office walls in 2021.
