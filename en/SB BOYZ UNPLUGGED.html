SB BOYZ UNPLUGGED

SB BOYZ UNPLUGGED

SB BOYZ UNPLUGGED is a reality series about the members’ training and daily lives before their debut as 1VERSE.

1VERSECONTENT

Training and making songs

Topics include HYUK’s vocal lessons, songwriting and recording with Andreas Ringblom, and the members’ basic dance training. KENNY practices Korean lyrics for Bambi, while SEOK has vocal-practice episodes. Stories about HYUK’s songwriting for Marshmallow and treating colleagues to a meal with royalties connect trainee life with music work.

Welcoming members

HYUK and SEOK prepare a welcome sign for KENNY and head to the airport. AITO’s arrival story connects Michelle Cho meeting him in Tokyo with HYUK and KENNY welcoming him to Korea. The series also introduces the four members’ first shared accommodation.

Trips and everyday life

Other episodes follow KENNY and AITO in Daejeon and shopping in Yeoju, trips toward the Taebaek Mountains, and birthday surprises. Self-directed vocal practice jokingly calls Papago a fifth member, while another episode has SEOK teaching staff English words. The final episode says farewell to SB BOYZ UNPLUGGED and introduces the next chapter for 1VERSE.
