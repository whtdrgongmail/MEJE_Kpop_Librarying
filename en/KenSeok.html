KenSeok

KenSeok is a fandom duo name for KENNY and SEOK of 1VERSE, formed by combining the two members’ names.

1VERSEKEYWORD

KenSeok · Name and usage

The spelling kenseok appears in fan discussions of favourite idol duos. The phrase Another KenSeok Moment also appears in an official content title, linking scenes of the two members with fan discussion.

KenSeok · Fandom duo names and reaction expressions

Seokenny and Bubuz are other names for the same duo. A fan introduction lists all three, while fan-work tags also pair Seokenny with Kenseok.

#congratsongettingmarriedkennyandseok · Duo names and a fan joke

The long tag spells out congratulations on Kenny and Seok getting married. It appears beside #kenseok as a fan joke about the duo, rather than a record of an actual marriage.
