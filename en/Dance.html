KEYWORD

Dance

춤

Dance appears in 1VERSE’s choreography practice, short videos, performances and fan events. This entry also brings together fundamentals training and members’ practice stories.

1VERSEKEYWORD

Dance · Fundamentals and classes

SB BOYZ UNPLUGGED episode 21 focuses on eight dance fundamentals. Episode 27 introduces SEOK’s video diary reflecting on his dance and English classes.

Dance · HYUK’s practice and recollections

Ordinary Person is a song featured in HYUK’s dance-challenge practice. SB BOYZ UNPLUGGED episode 37 looks back on how he first encountered dance and prepared for debut.

Dance · Taemin and AITO

SB BOYZ UNPLUGGED episode 54 centres on office talk about Taemin and AITO following choreography.

Dance · Movement

SB BOYZ UNPLUGGED episode 57 focuses on upper-body exercise and vocal practice with laughter.

Dance · WABIF performance and dance practice

WABIF has a Street Performance Video and a dance-practice video.

Dance · Dance in SECRET 1VERSE

SECRET 1VERSE episode 32 is titled Finalist AITO’s Dance. Episode 46, Kenny brings the orange filter to dance practice, connects KENNY with dance practice.

Dance · Random-play dance at KoreaFest

KENNY and NATHAN were announced for the performance and random-play dance at KoreaFest on March 28, 2026. AITO and SEOK were announced as not performing because of visa delays.
