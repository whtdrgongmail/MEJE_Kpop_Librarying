Cooking Kenny

케니의 쿠킹쇼

KENNY’s cooking show is the COOKING KENNY series of food-making with members and guests.

1VERSECONTENT

The first menu and a holiday special

The pilot features kimbap for SEOK and staff. The second episode is a Chuseok special preparing songpyeon under writer Moon’s guidance.

From boba to Indian food

Episode 10 asks what went wrong with making boba pearls. Episode 11 Part 2 prepares a Korean school dish with KENNY, HYUK and a chef. Episode 12 features Shahi Paneer and Ghee Rice in a story about making Indian food in Korea.
