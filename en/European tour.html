European tour

유럽 투어

A European tour is a series of performances across European cities. Destinations, schedules and fan activities differ between groups and tours.

1VERSETOUR

1VERSE’s European tour

The 1VERSE SHATTERED Europe Tour is a concert series announced for several European cities in 2026: Huskvarna, Paris, Helsinki, London, Belgrade, Frankfurt, Milan, Bordeaux and Berlin.

The programme combined performances with fan activities such as fansigning and fan meetings. The Milan fan meeting was introduced as the tour’s opening; London’s performance date and several ticket-sale dates were changed.
