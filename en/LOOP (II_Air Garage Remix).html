LOOP (II_Air Garage Remix)

LOOP (II_Air Garage Remix) is an album and a song of the same name by 1VERSE.

1VERSEALBUM · SONG

Songs and versions

LOOPLOOP (ARGO Techno Remix)

Album · Original and remix

The remix follows the original LOOP single, released on September 11. Its track, LOOP (II_Air Garage Remix), was issued as a separate release.

Album · Contributors

The arrangement credits name II_Air, Skylar Mones and Adrian McKinnon.

Song

This version was released as a separate single following LOOP. The original song takes its inspiration from the repeating cycle of anticipation, failure and trying again at a claw machine.

Song · Contributors

NATHAN, KENNY, AITO and SEOK are credited for lyrics and composition. The remix arrangement credits name II_Air, Skylar Mones and Adrian McKinnon.
