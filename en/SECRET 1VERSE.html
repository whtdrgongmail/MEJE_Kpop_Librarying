SECRET 1VERSE

SECRET 1VERSE

SECRET 1VERSE is a 1VERSE series presenting short moments.

1VERSECONTENT

Music and member combinations

Music-related episode titles include Chipmunk Rap, Ballad Time (AITO & SEOK Ver.) and Forest by Choi Yuree (SEOK ver.). Meet 1VERSE Japanese Unit and Another KenSeok Moment foreground member combinations.

Tours and fan participation

Tour-related titles include North America Tour Orlando recap and Choose your AITO (Tour Makeup Archive). An episode asking viewers to guess what NATHAN, KENNY and AITO were doing was linked to a prize draw among correct comments.

Short titles and playful themes

Titles such as AITO’s Japanese Snacks, Seok’s Cooking Show and Photobooth feature food and everyday objects. Sticker War: this is how it all began uses the sticker-war expression. The title about drawing animal representatives for the members includes the qualifier unofficial.
