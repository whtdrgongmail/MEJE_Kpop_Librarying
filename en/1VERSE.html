1VERSE

1VERSE

1VERSE is a K-pop group comprising HYUK, SEOK, KENNY, AITO and NATHAN.

1VERSEGROUP

Members

HYUK is the rapper, AITO is the dancer, and SEOK, KENNY and NATHAN are vocalists.

Debut album

The group’s first single album is The 1st Verse. Its tracks are Multiverse and the English and Korean versions of Shattered, exploring worlds coming together and the feelings surrounding a world’s collapse.

Later singles and remixes

In 2026, the group released WABIF (Wide Awake before I Fall) on January 21 and LOOP on September 11. LOOP was followed by the ARGO Techno Remix on September 18 and the II_Air Garage Remix on September 25.

The language of fandom

Fans name member duos and short reactions as well as songs. KENNY and SEOK’s duo is called KenSeok, Seokenny or Bubuz, while KENNY’s maknae jokes inspire the phrase maknae complex. GIFs combine the group and member names with reaction tags such as side eye and Caught in 4K.

A five-person Shattered performance

Shattered’s official performance video presents five members in lines and layered formations; AITO also appears in the choreography credit alongside two other contributors.

An everyday bus and office episode

BEYOND OUR 1VERSE EP.8 presents KENNY and NATHAN’s bus story through edited chat and an office exchange, connecting sleep, headphones, apologies and friendship with their words and reactions.

Danish snack tasting

The second SNACK SPECIAL video has AITO, SEOK, KENNY and HYUK sharing and rating snacks brought from Denmark. Sweet, salty, bitter and sour flavors connect with familiar-food comparisons and each member’s reactions.

A Chuseok special making songpyeon

COOKING KENNY’s Chuseok special has KENNY making songpyeon with Moon, with staff joining the shaping. White and yellow pumpkin dough, sesame filling, and star and full-moon shapes connect the cooking with their conversations.

Kimbap for SEOK and staff

The first COOKING KENNY episode follows KENNY making kimbap for SEOK and staff, with ingredient introductions, two attempts, SEOK’s tasting review and playful exchanges.
