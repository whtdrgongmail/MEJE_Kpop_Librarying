Kooky

Kooky

Kooky is the platform connected with city-selection presales and ticket conversion for 1VERSE’s 2026 European tour.

1VERSEKEYWORD

City selection and sales dates

Frankfurt was introduced as the top-ranked show in Kooky presales. The initial presale date of February 13 moved to February 17 after cost adjustments. Technical issues moved Bordeaux and Berlin presales to February 20 and general sales to February 23.

Converting city-selection tickets

Kooky tickets purchased during city selection were given a conversion process for the individual show platforms, using purchase proof and a single-use code per account. GA tickets for Helsinki and Belgrade were classified as conversion exceptions. The Milan fan meeting was introduced as the opening event of the European tour.
