Trying again after failure

LOOP begins with anticipation at a claw machine and repeated attempts after failure. Retro arcade sounds and glitchy electro-pop textures accompany the motif.

The language of coins and buttons

The song places coins, a button, a wallet and pockets alongside continued attempts. These small everyday objects become part of its imagery of repeated challenge.
