KEYWORD

5TARZ

5TARZ

5TARZ is the name of 1VERSE’s fandom. Pronounced stars, it compares fans to stars within the group’s universe.

1VERSEKEYWORD

Name and meaning

The name connects the universe created by 1VERSE with fans as its stars. 5TARZ is also written in Korean as 스타즈.

Ways of addressing fans

5TAR is also used to address an individual fan, while 5tars appears in an account of a fan event. KENNY uses several affectionate, playful nicknames for fans, including Little Twinkle Stars.

Star language used by fans

A fan guide announcement uses 5STAR to celebrate fans. A reply points to the #5TARZ spelling, while HYUK describes fans as the group’s sixth member.

A name drawn in chalk and formed by hands

Chalk-drawn 5TARZ and decorations lead into five hands forming a star.
