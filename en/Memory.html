Small memories and a promise about the moment

Multiverse presents small memories as parts of what makes us who we are. Near the end, it offers a promise to remember a shared moment. This theme sits alongside the video’s imagery of recording, including the camcorder and notebook.

Memory and a promise

The message of small memories forming who we are leads into a promise to remember a shared moment. Memory and a promise are two expressions of holding onto a relationship in Multiverse.
