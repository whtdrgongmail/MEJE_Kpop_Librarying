Snacks brought back from Denmark

AITO, SEOK, KENNY and HYUK taste snacks Michelle brought back from Denmark and rate them out of ten. Cheese puffs, caramel chocolate, crispbread, pork-rind snacks and licorice candy appear in turn. Their familiar-food comparisons, requests for another piece and contrasting ratings shape the episode’s humor.
