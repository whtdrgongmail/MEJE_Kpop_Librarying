Decorating with a first album

A fan who bought a first 1VERSE album describes deciding where to put its stickers, connecting listening to an album with collecting photocards and decorating belongings.

Sticker War

Sticker War is the title of a SECRET 1VERSE post. Stickers appear not only in album imagery and video decorations, but also in a content title.
