Headwords/HYUK
MEMBER

HYUK

HYUK

HYUK is a rapper in 1VERSE who has contributed to the writing of Multiverse and Shattered.

1VERSEMEMBER

Music

He contributed lyrics and composition to Multiverse. On The 1st Verse, his Shattered credits include lyrics and composition for the Korean version and composition for the English version.

Interests

His hobbies include talking with fans on b.stage and playing League of Legends. He also attends Pilates classes.

His favourite colour is black, and he described his MBTI type as ENFP in his self-introduction.

Fan expressions

Fans affectionately address HYUK as HYUKIE or compare his bright smile and energy to sunshine through expressions such as sunshine-leader.

Objects and symbols in fandom

Fan profiles introduce the tiger emoji 🐯 as an animal symbol for him.

Preferences and self-introduction

HYUK introduces Pilates classes, playing LoL and talking with fans on b.stage. His profile lists ENFP and black as his favorite color.

Waiting for teammates

HYUK recalls loneliness while preparing alone and relief when SEOK joined. He also calls fans the team’s sixth member.

A birthday wish for new members

HYUK’s birthday-party episode explores a wish to welcome new members.

New flavors described through familiar foods

HYUK compares the cheese snack with seasoned puffed rice and What a Date with jujube and mandarin peel. He calls the chocolate too sweet and says he cannot swallow the licorice candy, reacting differently to each snack.

Poetry and rap

His enjoyment of Korean-language classes and poetry led into rap-club activities and lyric writing.
