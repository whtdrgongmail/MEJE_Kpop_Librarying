From ingredients to two tastings

KENNY introduces ingredients, seasons the rice and rolls kimbap with a bamboo mat. Comments on the first attempt’s appearance and rice texture lead to a second roll with crab sticks, which receives a better response. Ingredient questions, pronunciation slips and wordplay such as Kenditional run through the cooking.
