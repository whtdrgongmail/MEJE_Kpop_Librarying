Plastic wrap is a kitchen item used in COOKING KENNY.

1VERSEKEYWORD

Glad wrap, plastic wrap, cling wrap and cling film

Covering the dough prompts a conversation about what each person calls the same kitchen item. KENNY says glad wrap, Moon says plastic wrap, and other staff offer cling wrap and cling film. A DIVERSITY caption accompanies the four names.

An improvised glove for kimbap

In the kimbap episode, KENNY wraps plastic film around a hand as an improvised glove, using it to hold egg while cutting and to mix or spread rice.
