Headwords/KENNY
MEMBER

KENNY

KENNY

KENNY is a vocalist in 1VERSE, with songwriting credits on both language versions of Shattered, WABIF and LOOP.

1VERSEMEMBER

Music

He contributed lyrics and composition to both the Korean and English versions of Shattered. His songwriting credits on WABIF are shared with NATHAN; on LOOP, he participated alongside NATHAN, AITO and SEOK.

Interests

He has described shopping for discounted groceries as a hobby and recalled being a teacher’s favourite in his computer science class.

His favourite colour is dark green, and his self-introduction lists INFJ as his MBTI type.

With the members

In an interview, he recalled going to a sushi buffet with the members and piling up their empty plates.

Fan expressions

He calls fans by several nicknames, including Little Twinkle Stars, Little Hot Pockets and Little Fruity Pebbles. A fan event included ranking these names. An early fan introduction also mentions chickpeas as a name for fans. Fans also describe KENNY as diva or address him with an elongated name such as Kenny Wennyyy.

Objects and symbols in fandom

Fan expressions include fake maknae and the golden-retriever comparison. Scenes with SEOK are also discussed under the duo name KenSeok.

Fandom duo names and reaction expressions

Duo names used with SEOK include KenSeok, Seokenny and Bubuz. Fan introductions also use the playful phrase maknae complex, while short reaction GIFs carry meme tags such as Caught in 4K.

Employee of the month and staff jokes

KENNY playfully calls himself an HR employee of the month. NATHAN says the social-media war with staff was also KENNY’s idea.

Showcase introduction

KENNY introduced himself as a personality hire at the showcase, jokingly describing his role as someone hired for his personality.

Expressions fans remember

Fans remember little tangerines, little soy sauce packets and the amvert pronunciation joke.

A birthday and a prank

KENNY’s birthday episode includes a prank involving HYUK and SEOK and a mention of TikTok filming.

Objects and fans around the tour

KENNY discussed onstage Funko Pops, earplugs for SEOK and his mother’s concert freebies.

The bus story with NATHAN

KENNY says he got off before NATHAN, who was asleep, and called his name three times. He also recounts telling the office that NATHAN might not arrive and jokes about calling seven times next time.

Another chocolate piece and toppings

KENNY asks for another piece to explain Daim Chocolate and gives it 10. He suggests cream with Knækbrød, while his Djungelvrål rating is shown as 1.

Making songpyeon as Khef

KENNY prepares dough and sesame filling with Moon in the songpyeon episode. Estimating quantities, pronunciation exchanges and the wordplay Khef accompany the cooking. A hypothetical crop question prompts his choices of pineapple and an apple tree.

Kimbap and Kenditional

KENNY calls his own cooking style Kenditional in the kimbap episode. He includes his favorite mushrooms, makes two rolls and receives feedback from SEOK and staff. A Nara Smith impression and a repeated line linked to TikTok punctuate the preparation.
