Window frames and skylights

AITO’s first Multiverse concept photo combines windows, frames, a skylight and a chandelier. Group photos use windows and skylights behind the members’ outfits. The WABIF Visualizer shows a window grid, plants and a white railing; filming footage places lighting stands and a cable reel beside windows.
