Singing Beetle

Singing Beetle

Singing Beetle is 1VERSE’s label, connected with the members’ debut preparation and music and video production.

1VERSEKEYWORD

Debut preparation and the office

SB BOYZ UNPLUGGED introduces Singing Beetle and Michelle Cho through the members’ debut preparations. Episodes cover HYUK’s arrival in 2021, first impressions around SEOK’s arrival in 2022, and KENNY’s journey to the company. Another episode features the founder and artist painting the office walls.

Music and video production

Michelle Cho of Singing Beetle is credited as producer for the Shattered MV and performance video. Multiverse MV and The 1st Verse jacket-shoot behind-the-scenes posts connect the label with the album’s release materials.
