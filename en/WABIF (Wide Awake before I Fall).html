WABIF (Wide Awake before I Fall)

WABIF (Wide Awake before I Fall)

WABIF (Wide Awake before I Fall) is an album and a song of the same name by 1VERSE.

1VERSEALBUM · SONG

Album

The single uses a Sexy Drill sound to express anxiety, hesitation and the feeling of being wide awake just before falling. WABIF abbreviates Wide Awake before I Fall.

Album · Music and artwork

KENNY and NATHAN contributed lyrics and composition. The album artwork was created by NATHAN and Yeonhee.

Song · Lyrics and sound

Its Sexy Drill sound expresses the tension and hesitation before a fall. The lyrics bring together wakefulness, falling, hiding and starting again. The title Wide Awake before I Fall names the moment where these feelings converge.

Song · Music and visual production

KENNY and NATHAN contributed lyrics and composition, with arrangement by Secret Weapon and Dr.Ahn.

NATHAN worked on the Color Coded Lyrics video and created the album artwork with Yeonhee. The Visualizer credits JIWOO KIM as director, WOOJI FILM for film and MOON for story development.

Song · Writing and filming

KENNY spoke about working with NATHAN and his attachment to a song he helped write. NATHAN described enjoying a relaxed shoot in which he could act without dancing.

Song · Different memories of writing

NATHAN recalled sharing audition experiences in an early session; KENNY described writing the chorus while listening to others, before Shattered.
