Headwords/AITO

AITO

AITO

AITO is a dancer in 1VERSE who has contributed to choreography and to the writing of LOOP.

1VERSEMEMBER

Dance and music

He contributed to choreography for The 1st Verse and spoke in an interview about working on the choreography for Shattered. On LOOP, he contributed lyrics and composition alongside NATHAN, KENNY and SEOK.

Interests and self-introduction

He enjoys drawing and has introduced himself as the only son in a family with four sisters.

His favourite colour is sky blue, and his self-introduction lists ESFJ as his MBTI type.

Fan expressions

Fan expressions include cat like and demure maknae, connecting his gentle impression with an animal comparison or an affectionate role term.

Objects and symbols in fandom

Fan introductions include his cat representation and the cat name Chimi. Moon earrings and mint-chocolate ice cream are also terms associated with him.

A fan’s story

One fan reading makes AITO a figure connecting different worlds in Multiverse and Shattered, linking parallel universes, robots and the camcorder.

Expressions fans remember

Fans connect aito please eat-o and 144 with AITO.

A first birthday together

AITO’s first birthday episode with Singing Beetle connects a surprise with his Birthday cover.

Art for WABIF photocards and touring

AITO introduced art used for touring; KENNY connected it with WABIF photocards and praised his design skills.

Choreography for Shattered

Shattered’s official performance video credits AITO with MICHAEL LE and 김한글 (Korean_cano) for choreography.

Different ratings for different snacks

AITO gives Kim’s Ostepops 3 and says 8 for the pork-rind snack. His What a Date rating is also shown as 8, revealing different reactions to different snacks.

Stages he hopes to perform on

He named KCON, MAMA and Coachella as stages he hoped to perform on.
