Headwords/NATHAN
MEMBER

NATHAN

NATHAN

NATHAN is a vocalist in 1VERSE whose work includes songwriting on WABIF and LOOP, album artwork and lyric videos.

1VERSEMEMBER

Music

He contributed lyrics and composition to WABIF alongside KENNY. On LOOP, his songwriting credits are shared with KENNY, AITO and SEOK.

Artwork and videos

NATHAN and Yeonhee created the album artwork for WABIF. He also worked on the Korean lyric video for Shattered and the Color Coded Lyrics video for WABIF.

Interests and self-introduction

He has described changing his university major from graphic design to computer science. He enjoys games such as Pokémon GO, names pool-table green as his favourite colour and lists ENFJ as his MBTI type in his self-introduction.

With the members

In an interview, he recalled making the members laugh by imitating the older members’ singing during practice.

Fan expressions

Fans jokingly compare NATHAN to Spider-Man or describe his addition as the group’s final puzzle piece. Some messages also express familiarity through yee haw.

Objects and symbols in fandom

His frequently worn green sweater is a familiar fan joke. Fan profiles also mention mint chocolate and Andrew Garfield’s Spider-Man among preference stories.

Reaction GIFs

Short expressions and reactions are also shared as GIFs. A reaction GIF named for NATHAN carries tags such as side eye and eye roll.

Showcase introduction

NATHAN introduced himself at the showcase as that guy from Arkansas, making his home region a brief, informal self-introduction.

Expressions fans remember

Fans recall aito please eat-o as NATHAN–AITO wordplay.

The Taste of Home birthday lunch

NATHAN’s birthday-lunch episode looks back on a surprise at an American soul-food restaurant.

Sharing work and home

NATHAN recalled his early WABIF session and shared Arkansas hopes and an Orlando flight anecdote.

From Growl to a closet writing session

NATHAN named EXO’s Growl as a song that drew him to K-pop and recalled working with its writer, including a six-hour closet writing session in Arkansas during a US fanmeeting tour.

Falling asleep on the bus with headphones

NATHAN recalls waking after the stop to find KENNY had already got off. Wearing headphones, he wanted a direct tap to wake him and discusses what a friend should do.

Learning dance and singing

In a debut interview, he described teaching himself dance and singing from online videos.
