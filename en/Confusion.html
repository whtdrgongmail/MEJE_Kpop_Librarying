Headwords/Confusion
KEYWORD

Confusion

혼란

Confusion is expressed in Shattered through images of a collapsing world and losing direction.

1VERSEKEYWORD

Shattered’s theme

Shattered’s album introduction describes confusion when one’s world collapses.

The Korean version

The Korean version describes feeling lost even on a familiar path. The world in its lyrics can evoke a sense of personal collapse.

The English version

The English version depicts losing direction and searching for home. Home can be read as a return point sought amid disorientation.
