Coloring songpyeon yellow

Cooked pumpkin brought by Moon colors part of the dough yellow. The finished cakes include yellow stars, and KENNY says he took creative liberty with the shapes.

A short-video costume

Alongside its use as a cooking ingredient, a pumpkin-shaped costume appears in the group’s short-form humor. The costume and the pumpkin used in songpyeon dough are different uses of the same headword.
