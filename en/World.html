Headwords/World
CONCEPT

World

세상

World is an image used in 1VERSE’s songs to express relationships and an unsettled sense of self. It takes different meanings in Multiverse and Shattered.

1VERSECONCEPT

Multiverse

In Multiverse, different worlds meet and form a shared world. Meeting and loving another person bridges their distance, making the world a space where individual lives connect.

Shattered

In Shattered, the world’s collapse is connected to confusion. The Korean version depicts a falling world and a breaking self; the English version uses a burning world and scattered thoughts.

Connections between the songs

Multiverse can be read as a world built through relationships, while Shattered can suggest the loss of what once supported the self. The same word thus holds both living together and losing one’s own world.
