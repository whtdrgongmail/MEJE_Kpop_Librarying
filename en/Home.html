KEYWORD

Home

집

Home is a place to live and return to. In Shattered’s writing story, it connects to KENNY’s wish to go home.

1VERSEKEYWORD

Thinking of home in the studio

KENNY says he wanted to go home after spending hours working in the studio while writing Shattered. The story connects the writing process with what he felt at that moment.

Searching for home in Shattered

Shattered (ENG ver.) uses imagery of losing direction and searching for home. The Korean version also includes an English expression about finding home near its end. In the English version, home can be read as a return point amid disorientation.

Different expressions in the MV subtitles

At the same moments in the Shattered MV, the provided English subtitles use fuel and home imagery, while the Korean subtitles refer to destinations, returning and wounds. The home theme connects to both song versions and their differing subtitle expressions.
