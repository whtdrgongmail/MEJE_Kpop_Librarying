Footwear expressions and lyrics practice

SB BOYZ UNPLUGGED episode 8 centres on KENNY’s Korean lesson about footwear. A Bambi behind-the-scenes episode introduces practice of the Korean lyrics of Baekhyun’s Bambi for a monthly evaluation.

Outings and introductions

Episode 49 connects KENNY and AITO’s shopping in Yeoju with Korean and TikTok. Episode 51 compares KENNY’s Korean self-introduction while meeting stray cats to a fan meeting. Episode 58 focuses on salon-related Korean practice with Michelle and KENNY.

A language quiz
