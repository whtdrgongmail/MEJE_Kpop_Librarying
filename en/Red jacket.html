The red jacket in WABIF

AITO’s WABIF concept photos pair a red jacket with blue hair, necklaces, earrings, white trousers and sneakers. The Visualizer shows a person in a red jacket and white trousers seated on a concrete ledge. The jacket also appears in a group shot from the filming behind the scenes.
