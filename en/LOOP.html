LOOP is an album and a song of the same name by 1VERSE.

1VERSEALBUM · SONG

Songs and versions

LOOP (ARGO Techno Remix)LOOP (II_Air Garage Remix)

Album

The single draws on the cycle of anticipation, failure and trying again at a claw machine. Its electro-pop sound incorporates retro arcade sounds and glitch textures. NATHAN, KENNY, AITO and SEOK contributed lyrics and composition.

Album · Remix releases

Two separate remix singles followed: LOOP (ARGO Techno Remix) on September 18 and LOOP (II_Air Garage Remix) on September 25.

Song · Concept and sound

The song begins with the expectation of winning something from a claw machine and the impulse to try again after failing. Its electro-pop sound incorporates retro arcade sounds and glitch textures.

Song · Lyrical imagery

Coins, buttons, a wallet and pockets appear alongside repeated attempts. The urge to keep the game going connects with the repetition suggested by the title LOOP.

Song · Contributors

NATHAN, KENNY, AITO and SEOK contributed lyrics and composition. The composition credits also include Skylar Mones, Adrian McKinnon and Jo Michelle; Skylar Mones and Adrian McKinnon handled the arrangement.

Song · Remixes

The separately released remixes are LOOP (ARGO Techno Remix) and LOOP (II_Air Garage Remix).

Song · Objects forming the repetition motif

Coins, a button, a wallet and pockets accompany continued attempts. The song’s objects express repetition through anticipation at a claw machine and trying again after failure.
