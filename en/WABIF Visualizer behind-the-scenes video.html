WABIF Visualizer behind-the-scenes video

WABIF Visualizer 비하인드 영상

A behind-the-scenes video featuring WABIF shoot preparation, songwriting recollections and the performers’ filming impressions.

1VERSECONTENT

Preparation and on-set equipment

The set includes a monitor, equipment cart, lights, camera and ladder. Makeup preparation uses brushes, cosmetic containers and a mirror; a hair-preparation scene shows a spray bottle. The video also includes a group scene with four performers wearing sunglasses and several jackets, and filming on stairs.

KENNY and NATHAN on songwriting

KENNY expresses attachment to a song he wrote, talks about working with NATHAN and describes it as one of the title tracks. NATHAN recalls participating in the work when he first came to Korea.

Props and filming impressions

AITO’s phone shows a blue-haired character drawing with Thing 1 lettering, while his white top displays NEVER MIND. At the wrap-up, NATHAN greets 5TARZ and describes enjoying relaxed acting without dance. SEOK corrects the shoot count from a second time to a third.
