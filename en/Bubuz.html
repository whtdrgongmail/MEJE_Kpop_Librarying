Bubuz

Bubuz

Bubuz is a fandom duo name for KENNY and SEOK of 1VERSE, used alongside KenSeok and Seokenny.

1VERSEKEYWORD

A name for the duo

Fans list Bubuz, Seokenny, KenSeok together when discussing the two members. A fan also playfully connects Bubuz with the Korean word bubu, meaning a married couple. The expression is one of several ways fans name the duo.
