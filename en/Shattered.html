Headwords/Shattered
SONG

Shattered

Shattered

Shattered is a 1VERSE song about the confusion of one’s world falling apart. The 1st Verse contains Korean and English recordings.

1VERSESONG

Songs and versions

Shattered (KOR ver.)Shattered (ENG ver.)

Theme

The song centres on losing direction as a familiar world falls apart. Its imagery brings a collapsing world together with an unsettled sense of self.

Korean version

The Korean version depicts a collapsing world alongside a breaking self. Losing the way even on a familiar path links the surrounding world with inner disorientation.

English version

The English version uses images of a burning world and scattered thoughts. Searching for home after losing direction can suggest a desire for a place to return to amid confusion.

Credits

SEOK contributed background vocals to both the English and Korean recordings of Shattered.

A fan’s story

Changing appearances in the corridor inspired a robot interpretation. One writer imagines two story paths, with AITO as either human or a robot.

Objects and spaces in the music video

A long corridor and circular interior form performance settings. A blue-cushioned chair, broken-glass-like fragments, green flames and character-like symbols build mechanical and fragmented imagery.

Disappearing light and breaking

Both Korean and English versions depict light disappearing. A collapsing world and breaking self, or a burning world and scattered thoughts, form the song’s fragment imagery alongside a shattered-heart expression.

Performance through choreography and space

The official performance video shows five-person formations and body levels across dotted lights, blue-lit white outfits, reflections and a circular building in black outfits.
