Headwords/SEOK
MEMBER

SEOK

SEOK

SEOK is a vocalist in 1VERSE. He contributed background vocals to Shattered and enjoys football.

1VERSEMEMBER

Career

SEOK is a member of 1VERSE and is listed as a vocalist in the group’s profile.

Music credits

He provided background vocals on both the English and Korean versions of Shattered on The 1st Verse.

Football

His profile collage lists football as a hobby. In an interview, he also described himself as a football fan and expressed a wish to visit Brazil.

Fan expressions

Fans express appreciation for his openness about his feelings with green flag Seok. The fanbook also includes a response recalling SEOK through the repeated greeting 좋은 아침입니다.

Objects and symbols in fandom

KenSeok is the duo name used with KENNY. The koala emoji 🐨 is also introduced in fan profiles as his representative emoji.

Fandom duo names and reaction expressions

Fans use several names for his duo with KENNY. Alongside KenSeok, Seokenny and Bubuz appear in fan introductions and fan-work tags.

Preferences and self-introduction

SEOK introduces eating rice with milk and playing soccer. His MBTI field reads CUTE (INFP), and his favorite colors are pink or yellow.

Showcase introduction

SEOK introduced himself with C U T E at the showcase, using the English word cute as a self-description.

A birthday-cake surprise

SEOK’s birthday episode follows staff and members preparing a cake surprise.

Members’ travel conversations

KENNY joked about earplugs for SEOK; NATHAN hoped to show him Arkansas. SEOK also encouraged fans’ dreams.

Describing salty and bitter flavors

SEOK gives the pork-rind snack 3 because it is too salty. He describes Lakridskugler as starting bitter like herbal medicine and becoming sweeter, and also says 3 for the licorice candy.

Tasting kimbap and giving a score

After KENNY feeds him the first roll, SEOK says it looks lacking but tastes good. He says the second attempt is better and gives it 60 out of 100, leading to KENNY’s sleeping-outside joke and SEOK’s laughter.

Starting out

In a debut interview, he spoke about his original resolve as he began his activities.

Memories of encountering K-pop

He recalled encountering Gangnam Style through a portable media player.
