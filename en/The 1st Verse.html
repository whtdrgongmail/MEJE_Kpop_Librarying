The 1st Verse

The 1st Verse

The 1st Verse is 1VERSE’s first single album, released on July 18, 2025.

1VERSEALBUM

Overview

The album pairs Multiverse, about different worlds forming a shared world, with Shattered, about the confusion of a world falling apart. The image of a world thus connects both coming together and collapse.

Track listing

The three tracks are Multiverse, Shattered (ENG ver.) and Shattered (KOR ver.). Shattered appears in separately recorded English and Korean versions.

Shattered choreography and performance

Shattered’s official performance video credits AITO, MICHAEL LE and 김한글 (Korean_cano), presenting the song’s dance through white-outfit light scenes and black outfits in a circular building.
