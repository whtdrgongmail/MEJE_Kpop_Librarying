The 2026 participation plan

KoreaFest was announced for March 28, 2026 at Agri Supply Expo Center, NC State fairgrounds in Raleigh. The plan named KENNY and NATHAN for the performance and random-play dance, with four members for other benefits. AITO and SEOK were described as not performing because of visa delays.
