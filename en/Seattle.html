US-tour cities and merchandise

The 2025 SHATTERED US tour listed Seattle, Detroit (Ann Arbor), New York and Bentonville. Detroit and Ann Arbor were paired with parentheses in that list.

Tour merchandise sales were announced to begin at Ann Arbor. The Seattle October 21 stop was distinguished from tour-merchandise sales, with separate merchandise information. The tour T-shirt sale date remained unannounced.
