From Chuseok to tasting songpyeon

Moon introduces Chuseok and songpyeon, then joins KENNY in mixing rice-flour dough, adding pumpkin color, preparing sesame filling and shaping the cakes. Their cooking is interspersed with conversations about plastic-wrap names and mortar pronunciation. They finish by tasting the cakes and practicing a Korean holiday greeting.

Ingredient names and tasting kimbap

When KENNY asks about an unfamiliar ingredient in the first roll, Moon identifies burdock root. He also joins the crab-stick naming exchange and the tasting conversation encouraging KENNY’s first attempt.
