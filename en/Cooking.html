Cooking

요리

Cooking connects food-making with the members’ conversations in 1VERSE content, particularly in the COOKING KENNY series.

1VERSEKEYWORD

COOKING KENNY

The series brings KENNY, other members and guests together to cook and talk. The process of making food and the conversations around it form part of the content.

Food and episodes

The kimbap episode centres on preparing kimbap for SEOK and staff. The Korean school-lunch episode follows KENNY and HYUK preparing food with a chef, while another episode introduces making Indian food in Korea. The boba episode explores the difficulty of making pearls.

Songpyeon and Chuseok

COOKING KENNY’s Chuseok special prepares songpyeon under writer Moon’s guidance, taking the series from its opening kimbap menu to holiday food.

Songpyeon and English tool names

In the songpyeon episode, Moon and KENNY prepare dough and filling and shape the cakes. Their cooking conversations include English names for plastic wrap, mortar pronunciation and wordplay such as Khef.

Making the first kimbap

The first episode has KENNY making kimbap for SEOK and staff. Preparing ingredients and trying twice brings feedback on appearance and texture, along with name and pronunciation wordplay such as Kenditional, Teaspulls and KEN-GREDIENTS.
