Stars are an image of the relationship between 1VERSE and its fans, connecting the fandom name 5TARZ with the nickname Little Twinkle Stars.

1VERSE and its fans

Fans are portrayed as the stars in the shared universe represented by the group. The fandom name 5TARZ is pronounced stars.

Little Twinkle Stars

KENNY also addresses fans as Little Twinkle Stars, carrying the star imagery from the fandom name into a member’s affectionate nickname.

Stars in the fandom-name announcement

Chalk stars and a star formed by five V-shaped hands extend the fandom name’s star imagery.

The shape of pumpkin songpyeon

Star-shaped cakes made from yellow pumpkin dough appear in the steamer. KENNY describes taking creative liberty and jokes about the varied shapes.
