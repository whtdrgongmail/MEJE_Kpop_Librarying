From dough to tasting

The Chuseok special follows KENNY and Moon making songpyeon: estimating flour amounts, adding pumpkin color, crushing sesame for filling, shaping the cakes together and taking them from the steamer to taste. Staff conversations run through the preparation.

Wordplay between cooking steps

The episode includes four English names for plastic wrap, mortar and pestle pronunciation, and cinnamon misheard as say no more. Khef and KEN-GREDIENTS play on KENNY’s name, while KENNY vs Staff groups playful exchanges with staff.
