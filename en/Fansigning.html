FAN ACTIVITY

Fansigning

팬사인

Fansigning is an event where fans meet an artist and receive autographs. On 1VERSE’s SHATTERED tours, it was offered as a fan activity connected to ticket benefits.

1VERSEFAN ACTIVITY

Fansigning with 1VERSE

The SHATTERED tours combined concerts with fan activities such as fansigning. The US and European tours each had their own participation arrangements and benefits.

US tour

The 2025 US tour’s 5TARZ of Honor programme included admission and soundcheck benefits under its companion arrangements. Guests accompanying a participant to fansigning did not receive signed items or group photos. Attendance and receiving those items were treated as separate benefits.

European tour

On the 2026 European tour, tier-based benefits were scheduled between 4 and 7 PM on concert days. Substitute fansigning attendance required purchase proof and an ID copy. Late arrivals could receive benefits available in the remaining schedule.
