Cover

커버

Covers present other artists’ songs through 1VERSE or individual members’ vocals and performances.

1VERSEKEYWORD

Individual members and a duo

Individual covers include AITO’s Birthday and Drip Drop, HYUK’s Move, SEOK’s You Were Beautiful, KENNY’s Purpose and Heartbreak Anniversary, and NATHAN’s Mistletoe. HYUK and SEOK share HOME IS FAR AWAY. The releases use performance, evaluation and practice-video formats.

Group covers and live performance

Group covers include ATEEZ’s GUERRILLA, HALAHALA and HALAZIA, Stray Kids’ LALALALA and Troye Sivan’s Fools. A Halloween video pairs Get A Guitar with HALAHALA. HALAZIA is presented as a live performance.
