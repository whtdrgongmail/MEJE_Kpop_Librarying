SHATTERED European Tour

1VERSE SHATTERED 유럽 투어

1VERSE’s SHATTERED European tour is a concert series announced for several European cities in 2026, combining concerts with fan activities such as fansigning and fan meetings.

1VERSETOUR

Cities and dates

The cities included Huskvarna, Paris, Helsinki, London, Belgrade, Frankfurt, Milan, Bordeaux and Berlin. Frankfurt was announced for April 23.

London was originally scheduled for April 12 and moved to April 13 for the health and safety of the artists and staff. The Milan fan meeting was introduced as the opening of the European tour.

Changes to ticket sales

The initial plan placed Kooky presales on February 13 and general sales on February 16. Following changes, general sales were announced for February 20 in Huskvarna, Paris, Helsinki, London, Belgrade and Frankfurt, and February 23 in Milan, Bordeaux and Berlin.

Technical issues changed the presale and general-sale dates for Bordeaux and Berlin. Belgrade was initially expected to open later than other cities; a subsequent schedule placed its presale on February 17 and general sale on February 20.

Tickets and upgrades

Tickets and upgrades had an Early Bird discount period. Final prices could vary with local taxes and platform fees.

Kooky ticket conversion involved purchase proof and one code per account. Helsinki and Belgrade general-admission tickets were treated as exceptions.

Fan activities

Tier-based fan benefits were scheduled between 4 and 7 PM on concert days. Substitute fansigning attendance required purchase proof and an ID copy, while late arrivals could receive benefits from the remaining schedule.
