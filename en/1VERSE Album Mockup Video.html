1VERSE Album Mockup Video

An album mockup video presenting The 1st Verse photobook, cards, disc and sticker designs.

1VERSECONTENT

The mockup setting

The video opens with a checked tile floor, neon-style lettering, ceiling lights, shelves and a display cabinet. Handheld-console-shaped and sword-shaped props and a metallic sphere fill the space.

Album and illustrated elements

An album package, circular disc and sticker sheet are displayed. The sheet includes drawings of a spacesuited figure, people, a bear, a ringed planet and lightning shapes.

Photobook and photo cards

Photobook pages appear with a group photograph and photo cards. The photos include figures in a white hat, several tops and denim trousers, and a HYUK label is visible. A composition brings together The 1st Verse title design, photobook, photo cards, disc and sticker sheet.
