SHATTERED US Tour

1VERSE SHATTERED 미국 투어

1VERSE’s SHATTERED US tour was announced for 2025 with concerts and fan programmes including fansigning, photos and soundcheck.

1VERSETOUR

Fan programmes

The fan programmes depended on ticket tiers and upgrades. 5TARZ of Honor included general admission, line access and soundcheck when accompanying an Everything or VVIP participant. Guests attending fansigning did not receive signed items or group photos.

Merchandise

Tour merchandise was announced for sale starting in Ann Arbor. The Seattle show on October 21 was listed without tour merchandise sales, while the tour T-shirt’s sale schedule remained undecided.
