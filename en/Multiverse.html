Headwords/Multiverse
ALBUM · SONG

Multiverse

Multiverse

Multiverse is an album and a song of the same name by 1VERSE.

1VERSEALBUM · SONG

Song · Theme

The song’s central message is that individual worlds connect through meeting. Rather than remaining isolated, they form a new shared space.

Song · Lyrics

The lyrics describe meeting and loving another person across different worlds. The distance between those worlds becomes the setting for a relationship.

Song · The meaning of a world

Here, a world can represent a space people create through relationships. Compared with the collapsing world of Shattered, the same image carries contrasting feelings of connection and collapse.

Song · A fan’s story

A fan interpretation of the camcorder connects NATHAN and AITO, reading the scenes alongside Shattered’s robot imagery as a parallel-universe story.

Song · Objects and spaces in the music video

The video shows a football being kicked and writing in a notebook inside a car. Dancing in the woods and a hand-binocular gesture depict bright everyday moments, while a blue video display later evokes recorded time.

Song · Keywords of the universe, shooting stars and connection

Starlight and a star map depict searching for someone; a shooting star connects with dreams and wishes. Galaxies, emotional connection and love across different worlds expand the song’s relationship imagery.

Album · Release

The single preceded The 1st Verse. The same song also appears on that album, released on July 18.

Album · The song

The song describes different worlds meeting to form a shared world. Starlight, galaxies, a map of stars and shooting stars frame its search for another person and connection between hearts. HYUK contributed lyrics and composition.
