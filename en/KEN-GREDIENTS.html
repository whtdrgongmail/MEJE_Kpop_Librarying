KEN-GREDIENTS

KEN-GREDIENTS is an ingredient label playing on KENNY’s name.

1VERSEKEYWORD

An ingredient graphic playing on KENNY’s name

This on-screen label appears in the songpyeon episode’s ingredient graphics, combining KENNY’s name with ingredients when introducing items such as rice flour and glutinous rice flour.

An ingredient list in the kimbap episode

The kimbap episode’s ingredient graphics list peppers, meat, eggs, kimchi, rice, crab sticks and seaweed in turn. The mushroom introduction highlights them as a favorite ingredient.
