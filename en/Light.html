Light

Light takes different forms in 1VERSE’s song expressions and concept photos.

1VERSEKEYWORD

Starlight in Multiverse

Multiverse uses starlight as an image of searching for someone in the universe, connecting a vast space with a meeting.

Disappearing light in Shattered

Both Korean and English Shattered contain expressions of light disappearing. Here light forms an image of disappearance, alongside Multiverse’s different image of searching through starlight.

Sparklers in night-time photos

Multiverse’s second group concept-photo set pairs a night-time setting with sparkler light and members raising their arms together.

Light revealing bodies in the performance

Dotted-light backgrounds outline bodies in Shattered, while blue light brightens white outfits and their floor reflections.
