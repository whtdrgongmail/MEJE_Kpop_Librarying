Shattered (ENG ver.)

Shattered (ENG ver.) is the English version of Shattered on The 1st Verse, describing a search for home after losing direction.

Songs and versions

ShatteredShattered (KOR ver.)

Lyrics and themes

The lyrics depict a search for home after losing direction and a struggle faced alone. A burning world, scattered thoughts and fading light deepen the confusion. Home can be read as the place the disoriented speaker hopes to return to.

Contributors

KENNY contributed lyrics, while both KENNY and HYUK contributed composition. Secret Weapon and Dr.Ahn handled the arrangement, and SEOK contributed background vocals.

Connection to the Korean version

The same album includes Shattered (KOR ver.). The Korean version describes feeling lost even on familiar roads and pairs a collapsing world with a self that is breaking apart.
